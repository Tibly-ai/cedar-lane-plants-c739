The two plans

Homes keep a quieter schedule. Offices need a louder one.

Both plans start with a walkthrough. We log every plant, note its spot and its light, then set a visit interval that matches how fast your rooms dry out. Plans are quoted after that walkthrough, based on plant count and travel.

Plan No. 01

In the home

For houseplant collections that have outgrown a casual watering can. Most households land on a visit every two or three weeks, with a key or door code left on file.

  • Watering to each plant's own schedule, checked by soil, not by calendar
  • Pruning, deadheading, and leaf cleaning
  • Rotating pots so growth stays even
  • Feeding through the growing season
  • Pest checks on the undersides of leaves
  • A short note left behind after every visit
Ask about home visits

Plan No. 02

In the office

For lobbies, studios, and clinics where the heat runs dry and nobody has time to notice a drooping leaf. We work early mornings or after close when that suits the building.

  • Weekly or biweekly watering routes with a logged plant inventory
  • Pruning, dusting, and tidy-up of planters and trays
  • Floor and tabletop plants kept on separate schedules
  • Swap-outs for anything past saving, handled quietly
  • Holiday and shutdown watering coverage
  • One point of contact and a monthly condition summary

Added to either plan

No. 03

Seasonal repotting

Root checks in spring and early fall, fresh mix, a size up only when the roots ask for it. We carry out the old soil and leave the floor clean.

No. 04

Light assessment

A room-by-room read on window direction, distance, and how long the sun stays. Plants get moved to spots they can actually live in.

No. 05

Pest treatment

Scale, mealybug, fungus gnats, spider mites. We treat on a schedule and keep checking the neighbors until it stops coming back.

No. 06

Plant replacement

When a plant is finished, we source a replacement suited to that exact corner, pot it in your existing planter, and take the old one away.

How a plan starts

Four steps, then we are on the calendar

Nothing is locked in until after the walkthrough. If a plan turns out to be more than your plants need, we say so and shorten it.

  1. 01

    The walkthrough

    We visit, count the plants, and write down species, pot size, location, and how each one is currently doing. Takes about thirty minutes in a home, longer in an office.

  2. 02

    The written plan

    You get a plant-by-plant care sheet, a recommended visit interval, and a quote covering the visits and any repotting or replacement we think is coming.

  3. 03

    The first visit

    Usually the longest one. We catch up overdue pruning, clear dead growth, treat pests, flush salt out of the soil, and move anything sitting in the wrong light.

  4. 04

    The standing route

    Same day, same window, same person. We log every visit and flag anything changing early, so a problem gets handled while it is still small.
