The plants stay. The worrying stops.

Most indoor plants in Nashville struggle for the same handful of reasons: too much water in winter, dry heat from a vent, a pot two sizes too small, and a pest that went unnoticed for a month. A regular pair of eyes fixes all four.

We keep a written record for every plant we look after, so the care plan follows the plant rather than our memory. If something is declining, you hear about it from us first, along with what we intend to do.

What a visit covers

Clipped from the care notebook

In the home

  • 01

    Scheduled watering

    We test the soil by hand before anything gets poured. More houseplants here die from a generous owner than from a forgetful one.

  • 02

    Pruning and leaf cleaning

    Spent growth comes off, leaves get wiped so they can take in light again, and pots get turned for even shape.

  • 03

    Repotting and soil top-ups

    When roots start circling the pot, we size up, refresh the mix for that species, and carry the old soil out with us.

At the office

  • 04

    Lobby and desk rounds

    One quiet route through the floor, usually before the first meeting, so nobody is working around a watering can.

  • 05

    Seasonal swaps

    Tired specimens are replaced and taken back to recover in better light, then returned once they have filled out again.

  • 06

    Pest checks and treatment

    Fungus gnats, scale, and spider mites get caught at the first sign and handled with low-odor treatments during the same visit.

Every plan is built around the plants you already own. Nothing on this list is required.

How it starts

Three steps, then we simply show up.

  1. 01

    The walkthrough

    We come by, look at every plant you have, note the light in each room, and check the pots and soil. It takes about half an hour for a house and a little longer for a floor of offices.

  2. 02

    A written care plan

    You get a plant by plant list with watering intervals, light notes, and anything that needs fixing first, such as a pot without drainage or a monstera that has outgrown its corner. Then we quote the visit schedule.

  3. 03

    Standing visits

    Same day, same window, every cycle. We leave a short note after each visit saying what was watered, trimmed, treated, or moved, and what we are watching for next time.
